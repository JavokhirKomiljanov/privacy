Privacy Policy

Instagram account @javohir_w2w · app “Jay OS Content Engine” · last updated 2026-10-12

This policy explains how Javokhir Komiljonov, founder of WIN TO WIN LLC (W2W, Tashkent, Uzbekistan), handles data received through the Instagram API for our own Instagram professional account @javohir_w2w. The app is used only for this account and is not offered to anyone else.

What we receive

  • Comments you leave on our own posts and reels: your Instagram username, user ID, comment text and time.
  • Messages you send to @javohir_w2w in Instagram Direct, and our replies.
  • Statistics about our own posts (views, likes, comment counts).

How we use it

  • If your comment contains the keyword we asked for (for example “Plus”), we send you one Direct message with the link you requested and a short reply under your comment.
  • If you write to us in Direct, the conversation is shown to our team in our internal customer system so a person can answer you.
  • We count how many people requested a link under each post to understand which content is useful.

We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising profiles, and do not message people who have not contacted us first.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on our own server in Uzbekistan and is accessible only to our team. Comment records are kept for up to 12 months; Direct conversations are kept while we are in contact with you and up to 12 months after the last message.

Data deletion

To delete your data, send the word “DELETE” to @javohir_w2w in Instagram Direct, or write to us by Telegram at +998 93 376 92 29. We delete your comment records and conversation history within 30 days and confirm in the same chat.
